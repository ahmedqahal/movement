## Fix phrases

- Writing appears anyway → `absolutely no lettering, numerals or writing on any surface`
- Too busy → `simple and uncluttered, only the objects described, plain background`
- Wrong angle → `viewed straight on, no extreme perspective`
- Too fantastical → `realistic, plain and industrial — not steampunk or fantasy`
- Tool looks wrong → describe its shape again in plain words; these are obscure tools and models
  guess when unsure. Getting the *impression* right matters more than exact engineering accuracy —
  the lesson text carries the precision.
