Privacy Policy

Movement — Watch Repair · last updated 18 August 2026

The short version: Movement collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no servers. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Movement has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no backend server. We do not collect personal data, usage data, diagnostics, identifiers, or location.

What the app stores on your device

The app saves your own content locally so it is there when you return:

  • Lesson progress and quiz scores — in your browser/app local storage.
  • Your Practice Log — the watches you record, including notes, tags, dates and any photos you add, stored in an on-device database (IndexedDB).
  • Preferences — such as your light or dark theme choice.

This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it.

Photos

If you attach a photo to a Practice Log entry, the image is resized and stored on your device only. The app does not upload photos anywhere.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual Practice Log entries in the app at any time. Removing the app deletes all of its stored data from your device. You can also export your Practice Log to a JSON file to keep your own backup.

Third parties

Movement includes no third-party SDKs, no analytics providers, and no advertising networks. It makes no network requests in normal use — the app works fully offline.

Children

Movement is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
