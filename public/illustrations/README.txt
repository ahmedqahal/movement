Drop generated artwork here. The app expects these filenames:

  mechanical.png    — full movement, top-down
  escapement.png    — lever escapement close-up
  balance.png       — balance wheel & hairspring close-up
  automatic.png     — rotor / automatic winding

Open the app's "Illustrated Plates" page to copy the exact generation prompt
for each one (important: generate them with NO text or labels — the app
overlays accurate, clickable labels itself). Landscape 4:3, 1600px+ wide.

After adding an image, use the page's "Position mode" to click-read percent
coordinates and fine-tune the hotspot dots in src/data/illustrations.js.
