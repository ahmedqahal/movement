# Illustration prompts — Set 3: replacing the remaining SVG diagrams

21 prompts covering every **static** SVG figure still in the app: the 6 technical plates,
the 14 in-lesson step figures, and the service-sequence diagram.

## Read this first — what NOT to replace

The app has three kinds of drawing, and only two should become illustrations:

| Asset | Count | Replace? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Technical plates** (geometry figures) | 6 | ✅ yes — prompts below |
| **Step figures** (small how-to drawings in lessons) | 14 | ✅ yes — prompts below |
| **Service-sequence diagram** | 1 | ✅ yes — prompt below |
| **Anatomy diagrams** (escapement, keyless, automatic, chronograph, quartz, calendar…) | 8 | ❌ **keep as SVG** |
| **Assembly / teardown walkthrough** | 1 | ❌ **keep as SVG** |

The anatomy diagrams and the assembly walkthrough **move and respond** — the escapement ticks, the
keyless toggles between wind and set, the chronograph starts and resets, the date ring steps, the
walkthrough builds the movement step by step. A static image cannot do that, so replacing them would
be a downgrade in teaching value even though the art would be prettier. You already have painterly
versions of those same subjects as Illustrated Plates — that pairing (animated diagram + painterly
plate) is the best of both, and it's worth keeping.

## Rules (same as before)

- **No text, labels, numbers or arrows in the image.** The app overlays accurate labels.
- Landscape 4:3, at least 1600px wide, straight-on view.
- Save to `public/illustrations/` with the exact filename given.
- Send them over when done and I'll wire each one in and place its hotspots.

## Fix phrases if a result misses

- Panels overlapping → `clearly separated with visible gaps between each panel`
- Writing appears anyway → `absolutely no lettering, numerals or writing on any surface`
- Too busy → `simple and uncluttered, only the parts described, plain background`
- Wrong angle → `viewed straight from above, flat-on, no perspective tilt`
- Too fantastical → `realistic, plain and industrial — not steampunk or fantasy`

convert it, swap the lesson or plate reference from the SVG to the illustration, and place its
clickable hotspots against your actual composition — same as the first sixteen.
